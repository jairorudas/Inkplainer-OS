# Paper textures

Photographic paper textures used as canvas backgrounds ("Paper photos" in the Canvas Background panel).

All of them come from [ambientCG](https://ambientcg.com) and are released under the **Creative Commons CC0 1.0** licence
(public domain: free for any use, credit not required, given here as a courtesy; <https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/>).
The colour map of each material was converted to an optimised JPEG (quality 84) and a 96 px thumbnail was made from it.

| File | ambientCG asset | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `crumpled-white.jpg` | [Paper003](https://ambientcg.com/a/Paper003) | White creased / crumpled paper (greyscale, seamless) |
| `kraft-fine.jpg` | [Paper004](https://ambientcg.com/a/Paper004) | Kraft paper with fine fibres (seamless) |
| `kraft-brown.jpg` | [Paper006](https://ambientcg.com/a/Paper006) | Brown kraft / cardboard paper (seamless) |
| `tan-smooth.jpg` | [Paper005](https://ambientcg.com/a/Paper005) | Smooth tan packing paper (seamless) |
| `cardboard-torn.jpg` | [Cardboard001](https://ambientcg.com/a/Cardboard001) | Torn corrugated cardboard (seamless) |
| `cardboard-worn.jpg` | [Cardboard003](https://ambientcg.com/a/Cardboard003) | Worn cardboard (seamless) |
| `white-paper.jpg` | [Paper001](https://ambientcg.com/a/Paper001) | Fine white paper (photo, used to fill the canvas) |
| `recycled-grey.jpg` | [Paper002](https://ambientcg.com/a/Paper002) | Recycled grey paper (photo, used to fill the canvas) |
